📋 Executive Summary

A high-volume week. Total jumped to 1,158 (+57.8% vs 734), almost entirely from Collections, which surged +77.1% to 935 (with a 442-ticket Wednesday spike). Support rose a more moderate +8.3% to 223 (WhatsApp Support 134, +10.7%; email 49, +8.9%; widget 40, flat). Collections spam was 261 (≈28% of Collections).

On Customer Support performance (over the 223 Support tickets), CSAT recovered to 93.9% (31 of 33 positive, +6.4 pp) — just under the 95% target, but a strong rebound from last week's 86.7% and on a healthier 33 responses. SLA rose to 97.0% (+2.9 pp), average net business minutes fell to 30.8 (from 36.3), and response rate climbed to 14.8% (33 of 223). The one soft spot was deflection, which slipped to 63.2% (−4.7 pp, below the 70% target) even as volume rose.

On Collections, CSAT improved to 91.7% (22 of 24 positive, +4.7 pp) while absorbing the +77% volume surge; response rate fell to 2.6% purely because the ticket denominator expanded (answers were flat at 24).

Four detractors this week (2 Support, 2 Collections), all rating 1. The two Support cases are the same seller CNPJ reporting an unavailable overdue boleto; with no response from Collections, the seller self-resolved both conversations and rated them negative — one underlying issue, and a genuine no-response gap to close. On Collections, both are substantive: #44047 (a buyer unable to pay a boleto) and #44441 (a complaint about collection timing on a not-yet-overdue balance).

This week also introduces the deflection split on the L2 tag breakdown (deflected vs human-path per tag), as requested. Quarter-to-date Support CSAT remains 95.4% (187 of 196), above target, with SLA at 91.6%; QTD deflection (57.7%) stays below the 70% target and is the standing gap to close.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.

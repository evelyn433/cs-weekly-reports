November 2, 2026 · Aira Weekly Review

Period: October 25, 2026 to October 31, 2026 · Comparison: October 18, 2026 to October 24, 2026

First edition. This report follows the metric framework agreed with leadership on October 30, in three pillars: efficiency (deflection rate), factual quality (error rate) and perceived quality (CSAT and SLA).

Deflection rate = conversations Aira resolves 100% independently (no human reply other than standard closing messages, and no human internal note) ÷ all Support inquiries, email included. Relay — when an agent tells Aira what to answer through an internal note — is shown separately as partial deflection.

The error-rate pillar is partial in this edition: it shows the error signals that can already be measured, until the definition of an error and the QA review are in place. Targets will be set together with the 4-week plan. Deflection and the L2 deflection split now live in this report instead of the CS Weekly Review. Full definitions and the baseline are in the Aira baseline memo (v3).

📋 Executive Summary

Aira resolved 30.7% of the 283 Support inquiries on its own (87), up from 27.5% the week before and in line with October (30.4%). The gain came from Aira finishing more of the conversations it handled (38.7% of touched conversations, from 35.2%), not from reaching more of them (79.5%, from 78.3%). Another 16.6% were resolved with relay support, 7.1% needed only a login approval and 24.7% were handled by a human. The remaining 20.5% were not touched by Aira: all 51 emails and 7 WhatsApp conversations.

By channel, WhatsApp reached 39.6% and the widget 31.7%, but 44% of widget conversations still needed relay. Email stays at 0%: it is 18% of this week's volume and caps the overall rate.

On quality, 20 technical-failure messages reached customers (13 the week before; 37 in the last four weeks), and 13 of 64 human-required conversations (20.3%) got no human reply within one business day. Five relay notes corrected wrong "ineligible" or limit answers.

The security filter blocked 10 conversations, including a message with only a CNPJ and an ordinary boleto request, and 48 conversations waited for a login approval. "I don't know how to deal with this case" fell to zero. Relay is still almost entirely one person: 78 of 79 instruction notes.

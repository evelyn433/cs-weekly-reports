What changed since edition 1

1. WhatsApp Collections is back in scope, as agreed with leadership on Nov 4. It has its own section and is never added to Support: its volume and profile are different, and a combined number would hide Support. Collections counts only real-intent conversations (no noise label and at least one customer message that is not an automatic reply).

2. "No human reply within 1 business day" now excludes holidays (Nov 2 this week).

3. Edition 1's Support deflection for Oct 25–31 is restated from 30.7% to 30.4%: refreshed data moved one conversation from "resolved by Aira alone" to "handled by a human". All comparisons below use the restated figures.

Definitions are otherwise unchanged; see the Aira baseline memo (v3).

📋 Executive Summary

Support deflection was 28.3% (56 of 198 inquiries), down from 30.4%. The drop comes from the mix, not from Aira: the Nov 2 holiday cut WhatsApp and widget volume, while email, which Aira does not answer, rose to 29% of inquiries. Among the conversations Aira handled, the share it resolved on its own rose to 40.3%, from 38.2%. WhatsApp reached 42.3% and the widget 30.0%, with 53% of widget conversations needing relay.

In WhatsApp Collections, Aira resolved 76.2% of the 328 real-intent conversations on its own (74.8% the week before). Boleto requests, three in four conversations, are deflected at 83.7%. Renegotiation is the weakest topic at 38.9%, consistent with the known bug: Aira agrees a renegotiation but cannot generate the new boleto.

On quality, technical-failure messages fell from 20 to 4 in Support (2 in Collections). 15.8% of Support human-required conversations got no human reply within one business day (9.1% in Collections). One relay note corrected a wrong answer.

On measurement, bot-resolved tagging is still not working: the fix agreed for Nov 6 was not delivered, so tag-based figures remain unreliable. The deflection rate does not depend on tags. The security filter blocked 6 Support conversations, again on ordinary boleto requests. Relay fell to 39 notes in Support (81 the week before), 97% written by Evelyn.

📋 Executive Summary

A split week: Support volume fell while Collections surged. Total rose to 1,261 (+57.2% vs 802), entirely from Collections, which nearly doubled to 1,030 (+98.1%) — about 40% of that increase was spam/noise (347 tickets). Every Support channel declined: Support was down −18.1% to 231 (widget 42, −31.1%; email 48, −29.4%; WhatsApp Support 141, −7.8%).

Support quality was excellent. CSAT was 100% (9 of 9 positive) — above the 95% target, though on only 9 responses, so read the level as directionally good rather than precise. SLA held above target at 91.1% for a second week, and average resolution time nearly halved to 46.4 min (from 100.7). There were zero Support detractors this week.

The standing gap is deflection, at 46.3% (+0.9 pp) — still far below the 70% target and not recovered from the drop tied to Aira's inconsistencies two weeks ago. Boleto requests remain the largest and least-deflected driver (86 tickets, 41% deflected); automating boleto retrieval end-to-end is the highest-leverage move on this metric.

On Collections, CSAT was 86.2% (25 of 29 positive, +4.4 pp) while absorbing the volume surge; response rate fell to 2.8% purely because the denominator expanded. All four detractors were on Collections (none on Support); only one is substantive (#47205, a debt-renegotiation request), the others being a bot-greeting drop-off, a mis-tap, and an automated boleto lookup.

Quarter-to-date Support CSAT is 95.4% (206 of 216), on target, with SLA at 91.2%; QTD deflection (55.1%) remains the metric below target. Note: last week's apparent L2-tagging dip was mostly CDC lag — on a fresh pull that week now reads ~92% tagged, so tagging is stable in the low-90s, not degraded.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.

📋 Executive Summary

Volume rose modestly — total +4.9% to 1,115. Support grew +17.3% to 251, led by the widget (58, +48.7%) and WhatsApp Support (147, +12.2%). Collections was roughly flat at 864 (+1.8%). The apparent −39% drop in Collections spam is a tagging-lag artifact, not a real decline (see the tag caveat below).

Support quality was mixed-positive. CSAT recovered to 91.7% (22 of 24 positive, +3.2 pp, still just under the 95% target). This week the dip is on the widget (80%, 2 detractors); WhatsApp Support was clean at 9 of 9. Both widget detractors trace to tickets that were not properly answered during the week — Aira showed inconsistency, going silent after the opening exchange (and in #51765 the handoff to Collections also went unanswered). SLA held above target at 91.1% but slipped −4.2 pp WoW and the margin is thin — 5 tickets are still open, and if all close late SLA drops to ~89%. Average resolution time was 53.5 min. Deflection edged up to 58.6% (+2.5 pp, still below the 70% target).

Four detractors (2 widget, 2 Collections), none left a comment. The one needing action is #51765 (widget, seller) — a buyer couldn't pay because the boleto returned "tipo de valor divergente inválido" and then expired: a payment-processing issue rather than a service one. The other widget case (#51294) was a greeting + CNPJ with no context; the two Collections cases are automated boleto lookups.

On Collections, CSAT was 93.3% (28 of 30 positive). Quarter-to-date Support CSAT slipped to 94.8% (275 of 290) — just below the 95% target for the first time this quarter — with SLA at 91.1% and deflection at 55.9%.

QTD average resolution time rose again to 131.5 min: 43 long-dormant tickets were closed this week (three August email tickets alone add ~16 min to the quarter average), so this reflects backlog cleanup rather than slower live handling — the week's own average was 53.5 min. L2 tagging is still catching up (68.5% this week, provisional), so the deflection level and the spam count are likely understated until tags settle.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.

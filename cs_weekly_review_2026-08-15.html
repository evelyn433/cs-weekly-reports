📋 Executive Summary

A lighter week on volume. Total fell to 734 (−5.4% vs 776), driven entirely by the Support channels: Support dropped −22.0% to 206 (WhatsApp Support 121, −22.4%; email 45, −25.0%; widget 40, −16.7%), while Collections grew +3.1% to 528. Collections spam rose to 154 (≈29% of Collections, from ≈28%).

On Customer Support performance (over the 206 Support tickets), CSAT came in at 86.7% (13 of 15 positive) — below the 95% target and the lowest weekly reading in this run. This is a small-denominator effect: with only 15 survey responses, each of the two negative ratings moved CSAT by roughly 6–7 pp. Both are real customer situations rather than mis-taps (detailed below). Every other Support metric improved: SLA rose to 94.6% (+1.9 pp), average net business minutes fell to 36.6 (from 76.6, roughly halved), and deflection edged up to 67.0% (+1.1 pp, just under the 70% target). Response rate was 7.3% (15 of 206).

On Collections, CSAT was 87.0% (20 of 23 positive, −1.9 pp), with response rate improving to 4.4% (23 of 528).

Five detractors overall (2 Support, 3 Collections). Both Support cases are real: #43064 (payment reconciliation — Pix paid to the BTG account instead of the boleto; request open 04/08–13/08) and #43058 (customer wanted a late-fee/interest refund, explained as not possible). On Collections, #43421 is a boleto/invoice case where the product and delivery side sits with the supplier, #43105 is a boleto inquiry with no further context, and #43090 is a rating-3 on a boleto-renegotiation request (boletos not yet due, and one due 07/08 not yet released by the system for renegotiation).

Quarter-to-date Support CSAT remains 95.7% (155 of 162), above target, and the week's broader operating metrics — SLA, resolution time, and deflection — all moved in the right direction. The negative responses are documented below and will be reviewed as usual.

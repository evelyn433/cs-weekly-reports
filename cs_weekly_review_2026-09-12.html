📋 Executive Summary

A shorter operating week — Monday 07/09 was the Independence Day holiday, with no Support operation. Total volume fell −13.7% to 1,088; Support was down −6.5% to 216 and Collections −15.3% to 872, with Collections spam dropping the most (−35.2% to 225). Note that 07/09 still shows 144 tickets — almost all Collections inbound (140); Support ran only 4 that day (see the daily table).

The headline is deflection, which recovered to 62.0% (+16.2 pp) — the closest to the 70% target yet, reversing the dip tied to Aira's inconsistencies. The improvement was led by boleto requests, which deflected 71.6% (up from ~41%) even as their volume rose. Aira appears back on track.

Support quality was strong: CSAT held at 100% (18 of 18 positive, on a healthier response base than recent weeks), SLA rose to 94.6% (+5.0 pp), and average resolution time fell to 43.4 min. There were zero Support detractors.

On Collections, CSAT improved to 90.3% (28 of 31 positive, +4.1 pp). All three detractors were on Collections, all rating 1, and all from the boleto self-service journey (one commented "Sistema complicado") — the pattern points to friction in the self-service boleto workflow rather than a handoff failure, since none were escalated.

Quarter-to-date Support CSAT is 95.7% (225 of 235), on target, with SLA at 91.1% and deflection at 55.6% — still the metric below target, though the last two weeks have pulled it up. QTD average resolution time (119 min) stays elevated by CDC catch-up on older tickets and the earlier ticket-hygiene cleanup; the weekly figure was 43.4 min.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.

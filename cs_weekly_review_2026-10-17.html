A lighter, holiday-shortened week — Monday 12/10 was the Nossa Senhora Aparecida holiday, with no Support operation. Total fell −22.3% to 706; Support −17.6% to 206 and Collections −24.1% to 500 (spam down −55%). Monday shows 78 tickets, almost all Collections inbound (71); Support ran only 7.

The big story is the SLA recovery: back to 92.6% (+29 pp, above the 90% target), reversing last week's collapse. Average resolution time fell from 186 min to 36 min. Both drivers of the miss eased: the median wait for a first human reply halved (180 → 88 min) and Aira's self-containment recovered to 37.9% (from 18%) — the first reversal after five weeks of decline, though still below the ~50% of early September. (16 tickets are still open, so the week may settle slightly lower.)

Support CSAT was 92.3% (24 of 26). Of the two Support detractors, one (#54690) is an automatic spam message — not real feedback — and the other (#54525) is a genuine case: a buyer whose Praso app showed no available limit while our side had them eligible, advised to contact Praso. Deflection rose to 58.3% (+5.9 pp) alongside the containment recovery, still below the 70% target. Collections CSAT was 90.0% (18 of 20); the one case worth acting on is #54739 — a buyer contacted about a debt they had already paid.

Q4-to-date (18.5% elapsed): Support CSAT 93.5%, SLA 75.5% (pulled up from 68% by this week, still weighed down by the first week's collapse), deflection 53.3%; Collections QTD CSAT 93.2%. Q1–Q3 remain frozen as the trend baseline.

The Aira containment issue is now ticketed for the Aira team (SUP-575, opened this week). One inbox note: Leads (inbox 23) jumped from 3 to 158 — likely a campaign or routing change, worth confirming with its owner.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.

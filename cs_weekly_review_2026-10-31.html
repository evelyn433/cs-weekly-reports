Incident — Chatwoot instability this week

Chatwoot was hit by an attack on Wednesday (29/10) and then ran unstable, with outages on Thursday and Friday (30–31/10). This disrupted agent response and resolution, and is a contributing factor behind the SLA slip (85.3%) and the longer wait for a first human reply.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.

📋 Executive Summary

Volume normalized after last week's Collections spike. Total fell to 802 (−30.7% vs 1,158) as Collections came back down −44.4% to 520. Support moved the other way, up +26.5% to 282 (widget 61, +52.5%; WhatsApp Support 153, +14.2%; email 68, +38.8%). Collections spam was 145 (≈28% of Collections, stable).

The headline this week is deflection, which fell sharply to 45.4% (−17.8 pp, well below the 70% target). The drop was broad across categories — boleto requests, the largest driver, deflected only 47% (down from 81%). This is consistent with Aira showing inconsistencies that are still persisting: some tickets have not been answered well, so more contacts fell through to the human path. The inconsistencies were not limited to Aira's answers — L2 tagging also degraded this week: about 13% of Support tickets went untagged (37, versus 8 the week before), concentrated on Thursday and Friday (see the tag-breakdown caveat below). Average resolution time rose to 51.9 min (from 44.1) and SLA softened but held above target at 92.9%.

Support CSAT was 88.9% (8 of 9 positive) — below the 95% target, but on only 9 responses: the single detractor (#45331 — a customer asking for a personal loan, which we don't offer) set the entire below-target result. Survey coverage collapsed this week — Support response rate fell to 3.2% (9 of 282), with WhatsApp Support returning just one survey.

On Collections, CSAT was 80.0% (16 of 20 positive, −12.3 pp) on the lower volume; two of the four detractors are substantive (a payment not identified requiring manual settlement, and a complaint about automated reminders) and two are ambiguous or already resolved, so the drop partly reflects low-N noise as well.

The Collections best-practices reminder is repeated below — tickets are still being left open without being marked as resolved. Quarter-to-date Support CSAT remains 95.2% (197 of 207), on target, with SLA at 91.6%; QTD deflection (56.0%) stays below the 70% target, and this week's Aira dip widened that gap.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.

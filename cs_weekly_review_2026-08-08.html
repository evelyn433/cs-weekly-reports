📋 Executive Summary

A steady week. Total volume was essentially flat at 776 (+1.7% vs 763): Collections recovered (+4.9% to 512) while the Support channels gave back part of last week's surge — WhatsApp Support 156 (−6.0%), widget 48 (−14.3%) — and email rose to 60 (+13.2%). Collections spam ticked back up to 142 (from 103).

On Customer Support performance (over the 264 Support tickets, excluding Collections), the picture held. CSAT was 96.3% (26 of 27 positive) — still above the 95% target; the single miss (#42840) is a blank rating-1 with no comment. SLA held at 93.7% (essentially flat), average net business minutes fell again to 38.4 minutes (from 61.6), and deflection reached a new high of 65.9% (+4.1 pp) — though still short of the 70% target. Response rate was 10.2% (27 answers from 264 surveys).

On Collections, CSAT improved for the second straight week to 88.9% (+8.9 pp, 16 of 18 positive) — its best reading in this run — with response rate at 3.5% (18 of 512).

Three detractors this week (2 Collections, 1 Support), none substantive: #42307 is a clear mis-tap ("Excelente atendimento"), #42789 is just "….", and the lone Support one (#42840) is blank. Effectively no actionable detractor feedback this week.

The continued improvement in deflection may also be reflecting the coaching work being done directly with Aira. We’ve been adding internal messages to tickets with guidance, corrections, and suggested responses, helping refine how she handles similar cases. While we can’t attribute the increase solely to this, the upward trend may indicate that these ongoing adjustments are starting to have an impact.

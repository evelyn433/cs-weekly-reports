📋 Executive Summary

The strongest Support week in this reporting run, on easing volume. Total tickets pulled back to 935 (−11.0% vs 1,050), with every channel down: WhatsApp Support 130 (from 148), the widget 40 (from 49), email 45 (from 55), and WhatsApp Collections 720 (from 798).

On Customer Support performance (over the 215 Support tickets, excluding Collections), every KPI was strong. CSAT was a clean 100.0% (22 of 22 answered positive, zero Support detractors), up from 90.0%. SLA recovered to 96.5% (+7.3 pp), well above the 90% target, and average net business minutes were cut to 24.2 minutes (from 63.9) — the fastest reading recorded. Deflection edged up again to 54.9% (+2.1 pp), still below the 70% target. Response rate was 10.2% (22 answers from 215 surveys).

On Collections, CSAT slipped to 73.9% (−11.1 pp, 17 of 23 positive) on a small sample, with response rate up to 3.2% (23 of 720).

All six detractors this week were on WhatsApp Collections (zero on Support), ratings 1–2, all buyers. One is a clear mis-tap (#40952, comment "5 estrelas" with a positive-sentiment tag); the rest were blank or "." with no substantive complaint text. CrediPay email captured 1 response (positive).

📋 Executive Summary

Aira handled 98.6% of the 219 Support conversations (WhatsApp Support and widget) and resolved 24.5% of them with no human involvement, up from 18.2% the week before and above the October baseline of 22%, but still below the 30% target. In Collections, Aira handled 73.4% of the 354 real-intent conversations alone, down from 83.0%.

The official deflection rate fell to 17.0% (from 37.1%), but this reflects tagging, not performance: only 24.1% of Aira's Support conversations received an outcome tag (36.5% the week before), and bot-resolved tags fell from 23 to 9. Engineering confirmed that the workflow that applies bot-resolved has had no recent executions.

Two warning signs this week: Aira sent 18 technical-failure messages to customers across inboxes (2 the week before), and 7 relay notes corrected wrong "ineligible" or zero-limit answers (2 the week before). Both map to plan items 3 and 4.

Relay grew to 101 instruction notes in Support (81 the week before), almost all written by Evelyn. "I don't know how to deal" handoffs fell sharply (8 vs 36 in Support), possibly because relay is stepping in earlier. 11.4% of Support human-required conversations got no human reply within one business day.

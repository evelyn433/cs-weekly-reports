What changed since edition 2

1. 27 widget conversations excluded. On Sun Nov 8, between 19:31 and 20:05, the widget received 27 conversations from a security test, not from customers. They are excluded from every Support figure below; details are being shared with security.

2. WhatsApp messages were lost during an outage (tickets SUP-690 and SUP-699). Sending messages was reported as failing on Thu Nov 12, and incoming customer messages stopped reaching Chatwoot from Thu Nov 12, 13:17, to Fri Nov 13, 16:42 on WhatsApp Support, and from 10:26 to 16:41 on Fri Nov 13 on WhatsApp Collections. The incoming messages were not recovered. This lowered volume; it did not inflate deflection.

3. Previous week restated with refreshed data: Support 28.3% → 27.8% and Collections 76.2% → 75.3%, because of agent activity added after edition 2 was published.

4. The figures now come from a fixed set of Hex queries, so every week uses exactly the same rules. Definitions are unchanged; see the Aira baseline memo (v3).

📋 Executive Summary

Support deflection was 43.4% (121 of 279 inquiries), up from 27.8%. Spam was three times higher than usual (27 conversations against 9) and counts as deflection; without it, deflection was 40.5%, against 28.0%. The rise is concentrated on WhatsApp, at 58.0% against 41.4% (54.5% without spam). Its cause is not clear yet, so it should be confirmed next week before it is read as an improvement. The widget reached 39.0% (30.0% the week before), and its volume doubled to 59 conversations, mostly sellers.

In WhatsApp Collections, Aira resolved 76.1% of the 343 real-intent conversations on its own (75.3% the week before). Boleto requests are deflected at 87.4%; renegotiation remains the weakest topic at 47.5%.

On quality, technical-failure messages rose to 12 in Support (4 the week before), and relay notes corrected 5 wrong answers (1 the week before). In one widget conversation, a buyer asked for a human four times and was not handed off; it still counts as resolved by Aira. In Support, 18.9% of human-required conversations got no human reply within one business day; in Collections, 23.3%, up from 11.4%.

On measurement, bot-resolved tagging works again when Aira closes a conversation, but Aira doesn't always close it (for example, after a customer's goodbye), so most new conversations still end without an outcome tag. Automatic tag-relay went live on Nov 16. The WhatsApp outage also hit the Leads line, which has had no incoming messages since Nov 12. Relay doubled to 86 notes in Support, all written by Evelyn.

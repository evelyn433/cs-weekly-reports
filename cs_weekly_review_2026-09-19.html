📋 Executive Summary

A flat week on volume — total −2.3% to 1,063. Support was essentially unchanged at 214 (−0.9%), with traffic shifting within Support from WhatsApp (131, −8.4%) toward the widget (39, +25.8%) and email (44, +4.8%). Collections eased −2.6% to 849; spam was 209 (~25% of Collections).

Operational speed was the standout: SLA rose to 96.6% (+5.0 pp, well above the 90% target) and average resolution time fell to just 24.1 min — the fastest in this run.

The concern is Support CSAT, which fell to 88.5% (23 of 26 positive, −11.5 pp, below the 95% target). The drop is entirely on WhatsApp Support (76.9%, 3 detractors); the widget was perfect (13 of 13). The one substantive case is #50211 — an explicit "muita demora no retorno" on a renegotiation that was AI-dismissed; the other two are automation-experience drop-offs (a bot-resolved boleto request and a dismissed menu).

Deflection eased to 55.6% (−6.4 pp) — but read this as provisional: L2 tag coverage this week is only 87.9% (vs a now-restated 96.8% for last week), and tag data settles upward over the following days (last week did exactly that). If coverage restates into the mid-90s, deflection should recover with it; if the untagged tickets stay untagged, it's a real dip. Boleto requests still deflected 68.8%; the weak tail is technical errors, reconciliation and renegotiation.

On Collections, CSAT was 92.0% (23 of 25 positive, +1.7 pp). Quarter-to-date Support CSAT is 95.1% (252 of 265), still on target, with SLA at 91.3%; QTD deflection (55.6%) remains below target. QTD average resolution time (125 min) stays inflated by CDC catch-up on older tickets — the weekly figure was just 24.1 min.

Reminder — ticket handling best practices (Collections)

As Collections agents start responding to tickets directly, a few practices keep the queue clean and the data reliable:

  • Keep an eye on your queue and respond to the tickets assigned to you.
  • When a case is resolved, make sure to mark the ticket as Resolved.
  • If you'll handle a case outside Chatwoot (e.g. from your own WhatsApp number), let the customer know via the Chatwoot contact that you'll continue on another channel, and then close the ticket — don't leave only an internal note with the ticket open and unanswered, even if you're continuing elsewhere.
